---

Backend engineer with five years of experience building payment and lending systems that stay fast and reliable under real-world load. Most of my work lives in fintech, where the margin for error is small and the traffic rarely sleeps.

I currently work at HungerStation, and before that I spent time at Grab, OVO, Sribuu, and Mozilla. I enjoy the parts of engineering that don't always get the spotlight: cutting latency, paying down technical debt, and making services easier to reason about.

### What I focus on

- Scaling high-traffic payment and lending platforms
- Cloud migration and infrastructure on AWS and GCP
- Fintech compliance, KYC, and observability
- Code quality, testing, and long-term maintainability

### Experience highlights

- Kept Grab's Paylater services stable under heavy Indonesian traffic using caching and database transaction locks
- Built the backend for an OVO lending dashboard serving over 3 million users and 50+ companies
- Reduced cloud operating costs by $300/month and cut endpoint latency by 50%
- Doubled monthly active users in a single month through a bank integration at Sribuu
- Raised Golang test coverage to 70% and held service error rates below 0.1%
